Company Overview: Hyder Electric Bikes is a Pakistani electric two-wheeler company headquartered in Lahore, Punjab. Founded in 2021, Hyder designs and assembles electric bikes locally with a focus on affordable last-mile mobility for students, commuters, and small delivery businesses. Hyder currently manufactures three models: the ELI 100, the HLI 100, and the SLI 100.

Model ELI 100 - Overview: The ELI 100 is Hyder's entry-level commuter electric bike, designed for daily city riding and college commutes. Motor: 1000W brushless hub motor. Battery: 48V 20Ah Lithium-ion (removable). Top speed: 55 km/h. Range per charge: 70-80 km in eco mode, 55-60 km in sport mode. Charging time: 4.5 hours from empty to full using the standard charger. Weight: 78 kg. Frame: reinforced steel frame rated for riders up to 110 kg. Brakes: front and rear disc brakes with combined braking system (CBS). Colors available: Matte Black, Pearl White, Racing Red. Warranty: 2 years on motor, 1 year on battery (or 500 charge cycles, whichever comes first).

Model HLI 100 - Overview: The HLI 100 is Hyder's mid-range hybrid model, combining pedal-assist and full throttle modes, aimed at riders who want flexibility between exercise and pure electric commuting. Motor: 1200W brushless hub motor. Battery: 60V 22Ah Lithium-ion (removable, dual battery slot optional). Top speed: 65 km/h. Range per charge: 90-100 km in eco/pedal-assist mode, 65-70 km in full throttle mode. Charging time: 5 hours from empty to full. Weight: 85 kg. Frame: aluminum alloy frame rated for riders up to 120 kg. Brakes: hydraulic disc brakes front and rear. Colors available: Graphite Grey, Ocean Blue, Matte Black. Warranty: 2 years on motor, 18 months on battery (or 700 charge cycles, whichever comes first).

Model SLI 100 - Overview: The SLI 100 is Hyder's premium long-range model, built for intercity travel and small delivery fleet operators who need extended range and higher payload capacity. Motor: 1500W brushless hub motor with regenerative braking. Battery: 60V 30Ah Lithium-ion (removable, dual battery slot standard). Top speed: 75 km/h. Range per charge: 120-130 km in eco mode, 90-100 km in sport mode. Charging time: 6 hours from empty to full, or 2.5 hours with the optional fast charger (sold separately). Weight: 95 kg. Frame: reinforced aluminum alloy frame rated for riders up to 130 kg, with rear rack capacity of 25 kg for delivery use. Brakes: hydraulic disc brakes front and rear with ABS. Colors available: Jet Black, Titanium Silver, Forest Green. Warranty: 3 years on motor, 2 years on battery (or 1000 charge cycles, whichever comes first).

Warranty and Service Policy: All Hyder bikes come with a standard manufacturer warranty covering manufacturing defects in the motor, controller, and frame, as described per-model above. The warranty does not cover damage from accidents, water damage beyond IP-rated limits, unauthorized modifications, or normal wear items such as tires, brake pads, and cables. Free service checkups are provided at 1 month, 3 months, and 6 months after delivery. After the free period, routine service is charged at PKR 1,500 per visit plus parts.

Charging and Battery FAQ: Q: How long does a full charge take? A: Charging time varies by model - approximately 4.5 hours for the ELI 100, 5 hours for the HLI 100, and 6 hours for the SLI 100 (or 2.5 hours with the optional SLI 100 fast charger). Q: Can I charge the bike at a normal home socket? A: Yes, all Hyder chargers work with standard 220V household sockets in Pakistan. Q: How many charge cycles does the battery last? A: Batteries are rated for 500 to 1000 full charge cycles depending on the model, after which capacity typically degrades to about 80% of original range. Q: Can I remove the battery to charge it separately from the bike? A: Yes, all Hyder batteries are removable and can be charged indoors away from the bike.

Test Ride and Booking FAQ: Q: How do I book a test ride? A: Test rides can be booked by visiting any Hyder showroom in person, or by calling our booking line. A valid CNIC and driving license (or learner's permit) are required for a test ride. Q: How long does delivery take after booking? A: Standard delivery time is 2 to 4 weeks after the booking deposit is paid, depending on model availability and city. Q: Can I customize the color of my bike? A: Yes, subject to color availability at the time of booking; custom colors outside the standard range are not currently offered.

Payment and Financing FAQ: Q: What payment methods are accepted? A: Hyder accepts cash, bank transfer, and major debit/credit cards at all showrooms. Q: Is installment or financing available? A: Hyder offers an in-house 6-month and 12-month installment plan through partner banks for salaried customers, subject to approval and a processing fee. Exact monthly installment amounts depend on the bank's current markup rate at the time of application. Q: Is there a discount for students? A: Hyder periodically runs student discount campaigns during university admission seasons; students should check with their nearest showroom for current promotions.

Return and Refund Policy: Booking deposits are fully refundable if canceled within 7 days of booking, provided the bike has not yet been assigned a chassis number for delivery. After a bike has been delivered, returns are only accepted within 3 days if the bike has a manufacturing defect confirmed by an authorized Hyder technician; in that case, a replacement or full refund will be issued. Refunds typically take 10 to 15 business days to process back to the original payment method.

Service Centers: Authorized Hyder service centers are located in Lahore (Model Town Link Road), Karachi (Shahrah-e-Faisal), Islamabad (Blue Area), and Faisalabad (Susan Road). Only these authorized centers should be used for warranty repairs; repairs done at unauthorized third-party shops may void the remaining warranty.
