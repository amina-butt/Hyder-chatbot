## 04. BATTERY & CHARGING MANUAL

### First-Time Charge Guidance
- **No Special Initial Charge Required:** Standard charging procedures apply from the very first charge. The battery does **NOT** require a prolonged initial 8–12 hour charge cycle like legacy battery technologies.

### General Battery Rules & Physical Protection
- **Chemistry:** Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) across all models.
- **Casing & Impact Safety:** Enclosed in a heavy-duty reinforced stainless-steel casing that protects internal cells from physical impacts, punctures, and road vibrations.
- **Removable:** No — batteries are fixed inside the vehicle chassis and cannot be removed for separate home charging.
- **Expected Lifespan:** 4000+ charge cycles (~10 years regular use).
- **Warranty:** 3 Years Battery Warranty.
- **Running Cost:** Approximately PKR 1 per KM.

### Voltage & State-of-Charge (SoC) Reference Table

| Spec / Parameter | 60V Class (ELi 100 / SLi Raahi / SLi Vibe) | 72V Class (HLi 100) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Peak Charging Voltage** | 69V–70V | 84V |
| **Floating Voltage (Full Charge)** | 64V | 78V |
| **Ideal Operating Range (20%–80%)** | 57V–62V | 73V–76V |
| **Charge Prompt Warning** | 57V | 72V |
| **Distance After Warning** | ~10–15 KM remaining | ~10–15 KM remaining |
| **Cut-off Voltage (Stop Riding)** | 52V | 67V |

### Charging Instructions & Safety Rules
- **Outlet:** Plug charger directly into standard 220V household grid outlets.
- **GRID ONLY WARNING:** NEVER charge directly from a UPS or solar inverter setup. Charging must be done using grid power.
- **Charger Safety:** Use only genuine Hyder chargers. If the charger fan fails, stop riding/charging immediately and contact service.
- **Storage Guidance:** If storing the bike unused, keep battery charge between 20% and 80% (57V–62V for 60V models; 73V–76V for 72V models). Do not store fully flat or at 100% for long periods.
- **Electricity Consumption:** Takes up to 2 units of electricity for a full charge.

### Rain, Washing & Water Exposure
- **Washing:** Clean with a damp cloth like a normal bike. **DO NOT** pressure/jet wash. Keep throttle and meter display dry.
- **Water Exposure:** Side covers must remain fitted. Avoid deep standing water or flooded streets to protect motor and controller. Water damage voids warranty. Never claim the vehicle is 100% waterproof.

### Gradient & Incline Guidance
- **Weight Ratings:** ELi 100 / HLi 100: up to 200 kg. SLi 100 Raahi / Vibe SLi 100: up to 175 kg.
- **Flyovers/Hills:** Designed to handle normal city gradients within rated weight limits. Do not promise high-speed performance on steep flyovers with heavy two-up loads.

## 05. FACTORS AFFECTING ACTUAL BATTERY RANGE

When customers ask why their range varies from the advertised figure, explain that real-world range depends on these 12 factors:
1. Rider weight
2. Passenger / additional cargo load
3. Average riding speed
4. Acceleration style (hard throttle vs. smooth acceleration)
5. Road conditions & terrain
6. Traffic congestion and stop-and-go frequency
7. Incline / flyover climbing
8. Tyre pressure maintenance
9. Weather and ambient temperature
10. Battery state of health
11. Selected riding mode
12. Frequency of starts from a complete standstill
